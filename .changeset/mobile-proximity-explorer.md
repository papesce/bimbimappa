Mobile proximity explorer: from any saved place you can now explore kid-friendly places nearby on mobile — a one-tap "Explore nearby" action surfaces a list of nearby places you can browse and jump between on the map. The mobile browse flow was also refined: the filter context is restored in the header, active filters are shown as chips on the map, the mobile map context controls were fixed, and place actions were consolidated into a single bottom-sheet surface (primary source, navigation links, and delete inline instead of a separate More menu).
